 PART 6 - AI RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signed in as student. All of this uses Badminton, set up by the seed.

[ ] 6.1  SAME FACILITY, OTHER TIMES
         Badminton -> Court 1 -> TOMORROW, 15:00, 60 min, 2 people.
         Press Check availability & book.
         Expected: "That time is taken - AI-ranked alternatives".
         4 cards, one marked "Best", each with reasons such as
         "close to your preferred time" or "off-peak, likely quieter".
         Seed-verified result, roughly:
           Court 1 2:00 pm   Court 1 4:00 pm   Court 2 3:00 pm   Court 3 3:00 pm

[ ] 6.2  OTHER FACILITIES OF THE SAME TYPE
         Expected: Court 2 AND Court 3 appear at 3:00 pm, labelled with
         the court name and the reason "same type of facility".
         Their scores are within 0.002 of the top cards - a near tie.
         Before any learning, the AI very slightly favours slots on the
         court you picked; this evens out as it learns (see 6.6).

[ ] 6.3  NEVER A DIFFERENT CATEGORY OR A CLOSED COURT
         Expected: no futsal court, and no Court 4 (maintenance).

[ ] 6.4  BOOK A SUGGESTION ON ANOTHER COURT
         Click the Court 2 3:00 pm card.
         Expected: the success message ends "- Court 2", and My bookings
         shows Court 2, not Court 1.

[ ] 6.5  LOOK-AHEAD WHEN THE DAY IS FULL
         Badminton -> Court 1 -> the FULL DAY date, 15:00, 60 min.
         Expected: a message "<that day> is full everywhere - showing the
         next days with space", and cards for the FOLLOWING day with the
         reason "nearest day with space". No setup needed - the seed
         booked every court all day.

[ ] 6.5b COURT 3 KEEPS ITS OWN RULES
         Badminton -> Court 1 -> TOMORROW, 19:00, 60 min.
         Expected: if Court 3 appears, never later than 7:00 pm start
         (it closes at 20:00, Courts 1-2 close at 22:00).

[ ] 6.5c EQUIPMENT STOCK
         Badminton -> Court 2 -> TOMORROW, 15:00, 60 min, request
         2 Rackets.
         Expected: refused - "Only 1 of "Racket" available at that time".
         Request 1 Racket instead: accepted.

[ ] 6.6  THE AI LEARNS
         Supabase -> Table editor -> profiles -> the demo student's row.
         Note the rec_bandit column (empty at first).
         Accept a suggestion (click one of the cards).
         Refresh the table.
         Expected: rec_bandit now holds numbers, and "n" has gone up.
         Accept a few more; "n" keeps rising.

[ ] 6.7  EVALUATION LOGGING
         Supabase -> evaluation_events.
         Expected: a "rec_shown" row for each list you saw and a
         "rec_accepted" row for each card you clicked. The note column
         says whether it was another facility and how many days ahead.

[ ] 6.8  PEAK HOURS - LEARNED vs DEFAULT
         Rule: under 30 bookings in a category -> campus defaults
         (12-2 pm and 5-8 pm). 30 or more -> learned from history.
         a. After 6.1, open evaluation_events. The newest rec_shown note
            should say "peaks=learned" (badminton has 106 bookings; the

            off-peak - unlike the defaults).
         b. Try a clash on the futsal court, then check again.
            Expected: "peaks=default" (futsal only has 12 bookings).
