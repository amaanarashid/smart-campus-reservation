1. LIVE BOOKING PAGES - real grids, no login to view
------------------------------------------------------------

Sunway University - Group Project Pods          [START HERE]
https://sunway.libcal.com/reserve/gpp
  Your evidence. Above the calendar it prints:
  - "Group size, minimum 3 to maximum of 10 persons
     (For POD 1-5) and minimum 3 to maximum of 6 persons
     (For POD 6-11)."
  - "Users must show up at the Service Counter (Ground
     Level) within the first 15 minutes of the booked
     timeslot. If not, the bookings will be considered
     forfeited and may be cancelled."
  SCREENSHOT THIS with the date visible. It is Appendix F.

Monash University Malaysia
https://monash.libcal.com/
  The other Malaysian LibCal deployment.

University of the Pacific
https://pacific.libcal.com/spaces
  15-minute forfeiture policy.

Camosun College
https://camosun.libcal.com/reserve/LANstudyrooms
  Same pattern, different continent.

College of Charleston
https://libcal.charleston.edu/reserve/studyrooms

University of Florida - Library West
https://librarywest.uflib.ufl.edu/spaces/study-rooms

Duke Law Library
https://law.duke.edu/lib/studyrooms/
  Policy page rather than a grid.

  WHAT TO NOTICE: they are all near-identical. One product,
  configured seven ways, and every one of them bolts on
  policy text that the software does not enforce.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. VENDOR DEMOS - you can actually drive these
------------------------------------------------------------

MIDAS - online demo                     [MOST IMPORTANT ONE]
https://mid.as/online-demo
  Your closest competitor. Look for Booking Alternatives:
  request a slot that is taken and see what it offers.
  Then notice what it does NOT do - the alternatives are
  not ranked, and nothing weighs group size against room
  size. That is your objective 3.

MIDAS - free trial
https://mid.as/free-trial
  If the demo does not expose alternatives, the trial will.
  You can also configure the substitution table by hand,
  which is exactly the limitation you are arguing against.

InnoSoft Fusion - education demo portal
https://edudemo.innosoftfusion.com/
https://release401.innosoftfusion.com/Facility
  The campus recreation segment. Courts, memberships,
  access control.

Skedda
https://www.skedda.com/
  Free trial. Look at how conditional booking rules are
  configured - space, duration, time of day, user tag.
